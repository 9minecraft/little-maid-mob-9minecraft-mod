# Little Maid Mob — Recruit Cute Anime Helpers

Little Maid Mob 9minecraft Mod brings anime helpers and flying fairies into your world. You can tame these helpers with sweet treats, give them daily chores, and build a cozy home together. They can fight enemies, harvest crops, and keep your chests tidy while you wander around. The mod also adds interactive furniture, shrines, and board games to enjoy during your downtime.

Summon maids at the shrine altar and tame them by offering a cake.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Snownee**, **Succinum**, **TartaricAcid**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
